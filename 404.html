Enhancing Emergency Response and Traffic Safety in San Rafael

When incidents clog the 101 Freeway, city streets quickly back up — slowing emergency response and risking public safety. To fix this, the San Rafael Police Department turned to Miovision for a smarter solution.

56

The Waterloo-Kitchener LRT (ION) uses Opticom Transit Signal Priority (TSP) to stay on schedule, minimize delays, and cut emissions—delivering a better transit experience for riders across the region.
